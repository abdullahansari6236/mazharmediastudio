Questions before you start?

Can't find your answer here? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll reply within a few hours.

How fast will I get my first draft?

Thumbnails are usually ready in 24 to 48 hours. Video timelines depend on length and style; a typical brand promo is delivered in under 5 days. Tell us your deadline and we'll plan around it.

How do revisions work?

You receive a draft, send us your notes, and we refine it until it matches your vision. You are never handed a final file you are not happy with.

What do I receive at delivery?

Final files in every format you need. Logos come as SVG, PNG and PDF. Videos can be cut for each platform in 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1.

Can you run my channel for me?

Yes. Our account management covers uploads, scheduling, SEO-optimized titles, tags and descriptions, thumbnails and community replies, so you can focus on filming.
